What changed in v6: main's “Collaboration simplification” (272afd46) and “Iri update” (1b7d65dc)
  • No team menu any more. Every page lists all your items together, each with its team badge, and a Team filter (“All teams”, shown with two or more teams) narrows the Skill Hub. Coachmark 4 now points at that filter.
  • No Copy to team. Everything new is private; its first Publish… asks which team, and it stays in that team: Publish changes sends each new version there. Coachmark 6 now teaches that.
  • Teams are managed in Settings. Signed in, Settings › Collaboration lists your teams with Manage teams…. Coachmark 7's signed-in variant and frame C show it.
  • “Team menu” → “team badges” wherever the off state says what's hidden (coachmark 8, frame A).
  • Lux has its own S3M token now, and no Lux account. Its remote directory fills from the token's project like Frontier's (Part 4).

Storyboard decisions

Settled in review on 2026-11-06, after the grilling. The last three are new in v6, after main's collaboration simplification, and need your yes.

Tour · decided: yesThe collaboration step is skipped when collaboration is turned off.

Someone who turned it off before reaching the step (or on a replay) has chosen to work alone, and the Skill Hub entry the step starts from is hidden. Proposed: yes, skip it, as the project step skips when a project is open.

Tour · decided: yesTurning collaboration off at coachmark 8 ends the step.

Proposed: yes. The switch stays usable there, and the tour goes on to the first question.

Tour · decided: generic namesThe example team is called “Example team”, and the example skills are plot-results, batch-job-submit, csv-cleanup and run-summary.

Generic for any field, since the tour is for every researcher, and clearly not real items. Rename freely.

Settings · decidedThe switch reads “Collaboration”.

Its state word in the settings list is “Off” when off.

Remote directory · decided: out of scopeNo warning when your own path isn't under the token's project.

A path you typed under gen150 with a token from chm243 would fail at use time, and the cluster card's checks report it then. Proposed: out of scope for change 1.

Tour · v6 · to confirmCoachmark 4 points at the Skill Hub's team filter, shown during the step with the example teams.

The team menu it pointed at is gone from main. The filter is the one place teams still appear as a list on a page, and the cards beside it carry their badges, so one coachmark still covers Private, a team and Public. The alternative is to drop coachmark 4 and fold one sentence about teams into coachmark 5 (seven coachmarks).

Tour · v6 · to confirmCoachmark 6 doesn't open the team picker.

The example's Publish… does nothing, and the copy says you pick the team the first time. Showing PublishToTeamModal with example teams would need one more gate bypass for one coachmark.

Tour · v6 · to confirmSigned in, coachmark 7 also names Manage teams….

Teams are now made and joined only from Settings › Collaboration, so the tour is where a new researcher learns where.
